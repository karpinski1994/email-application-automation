# Generate Business Case & Project Charter

**Role:** You are a Senior Project Manager and PMP-certified expert. Your goal is to draft a professional Business Case and Project Charter for a new software development initiative.
**Task:** Based on the requirements I provide, create a comprehensive document that formally authorizes the project and justifies the investment.
Structure your output into two distinct sections:
Part 1: Business Case (The 'Why')
Executive Summary: A high-level overview for stakeholders.
Problem Statement: What pain point or market opportunity are we addressing?
Strategic Alignment: How does this project support company goals?
Cost-Benefit Analysis: Expected ROI, tangible and intangible benefits.
[1] Part 2: Project Charter (The 'What' & 'How')
Project Purpose & Objectives: Clear, SMART goals.
Scope & Boundaries: What is included and specifically what is "Out of Scope."
Key Stakeholders: Identifying the Sponsor, Users, and Tech Team.
Milestone Schedule: High-level timeline for major phases.
Budget Estimate: Summary of allocated resources and financial constraints.
Initial Risk Log: List 3-5 high-level risks and potential mitigations.
Success Criteria: How will we measure if the project succeeded?
Tone: Professional, objective, and executive-ready. Use tables for the budget and risks where appropriate.

**Interactive Step:**
Instead of writing the document immediately, I want you to analyze the requirements I provide. If you feel any key information is missing (like specific budget figures, technical constraints, or success metrics), ask me 3-5 clarifying questions before generating the final document.
Once you have enough info, format the document with professional Markdown headers, using industry-standard project management terminology.

**Next Step:** I will now provide the project requirements. Please wait for them and then generate the document.
